# XG Capital Strategies

**Applied research for decision-making under uncertainty.**

XG Capital Strategies is an applied research and systems organization studying how useful structure emerges in complex, dynamic environments and how that structure can improve prediction, coordination, control, and decision-making.

We build experimental systems, test them against reality, preserve what survives, document what fails, and selectively turn validated research into durable tools and products.

Financial markets remain one of our primary proving grounds because they are noisy, adversarial, adaptive, and measurable. They are not the boundary of the research.

## What we study

Our work clusters around a few recurring questions:

- **Prediction:** What information in a complex system is genuinely predictive, and what only looks predictive in sample?
- **Dynamics:** How do regimes, cycles, feedback loops, relationships, and state transitions evolve over time?
- **Coordination:** How can people and AI systems represent intent, context, capabilities, and opportunities without flattening away privacy or nuance?
- **Agency:** How can autonomous systems act usefully while remaining inside explicit human-defined authority boundaries?
- **Adaptation:** When should a system reuse, recombine, or repurpose existing capabilities instead of generating a new solution from scratch?
- **Reasoning:** How can conclusions remain inspectable, source-linked, challengeable, and bounded by the evidence that produced them?

We do not assume these questions belong to one industry. The same underlying problems appear in markets, software, organizations, networks, language, and other evolving systems.

## Selected research and systems

### Heatmap Strategy Lab

A cross-asset market-field research environment for testing what relational market structure can and cannot predict. The lab emphasizes out-of-sample validation, regime testing, delay and transaction-cost survival, and durable recording of null results as well as positive findings.

### Valence

A conversation-first coordination system that helps people express what they are trying to make happen, what they can contribute, and where useful collaboration paths may exist. Its broader research direction includes privacy-preserving network intelligence and user-owned context.

### Portable Human Context

A proposed vendor-neutral standard for representing useful human context in a machine-readable, inspectable, selectively shareable form. The goal is to let people move between AI systems without repeatedly rebuilding the context that makes those systems useful.

### Lightcone

A control plane for autonomous AI systems. Lightcone treats delegated authority as a deterministic execution boundary: what an agent may do, with which tools and data, for how long, at what cost, and under what approval conditions.

### Exaptly / Capability Substrate Research

Research into a pre-generation software layer that can discover, verify, compose, and repurpose existing software capabilities before an AI system spends inference recreating them. The underlying thesis is that AI-generated software should become reusable infrastructure rather than disposable digital detritus.

### PhaseStyle

Dynamical stylometry research exploring authorship, stylistic drift, recurrence, and change points through phase-space and time-series methods.

## How we work

Our default path is:

```text
Observation
    ↓
Hypothesis
    ↓
Experiment
    ↓
Falsification / validation
    ↓
Durable research record
    ↓
System
    ↓
Product, standard, or further research
```

A promising chart, model output, or backtest is not enough. Claims should become stronger only as they survive stronger tests.

That means we value:

- out-of-sample and walk-forward evaluation;
- explicit baselines and appropriate benchmarks;
- delay, cost, and implementation realism;
- reproducible experiments where practical;
- provenance for data and conclusions;
- clear separation between observation, inference, and speculation;
- retaining failed hypotheses instead of quietly deleting them.

**Null results are research outputs.** Knowing where structure does not exist narrows the search for where it does.

## Research, IP, and productization

XG Capital Strategies is the research and intellectual-property layer for much of this work. **Kairos Dynamics** is the commercialization and productization layer for selected systems that are ready to become products, platforms, or operating businesses.

Not every XGCS experiment is intended to become a company, and not every useful result should be forced into a product roadmap. Research is allowed to remain research.

## Open and private work

Much of our active work is private while ideas, interfaces, security boundaries, and evidence standards are still changing quickly. Core proprietary trading logic, sensitive infrastructure, private data, partner information, credentials, and trade-secret research are not published merely because adjacent work is discussed publicly.

Where openness improves interoperability or advances the research, we intend to release selected specifications, tools, research artifacts, and reference implementations when they are mature enough to be useful rather than misleading.
